# FXPS7400D4S

The FXPS7400D4S high-performance, high-precision barometric absolute 
pressure (BAP) sensor consists of a compact capacitive micro-electro-mechanical 
systems (MEMS) device coupled with a digital integrated circuit (IC) producing a 
fully calibrated digital output.

The FXPS7400D4S pressure sensor family is designed for robust industrial and automotive
applications, offering reliable performance across a wide operating temperature 
range from –40°C to 130°C. It integrates a digital self-test function to support 
diagnostics and functional safety requirements, and features a 32-bit SPI-compatible 
serial interface for fast, accurate digital communication. 

This sensor delivers highly accurate pressure and temperature readings 
through either a serial peripheral interface (SPI) or an inter-integrated 
circuit (I2C) interface. The FXPS7400D4S uses either a 3.3 V or 5.0 V power supply. 
Furthermore, the sensor employs an on-demand digital self-test for the digital IC 
and the MEMS transducers. Note that this sensor family includes three variants, 
each implemented as a separate PN (chip).
 FXPS7400DI4S ----- I2C
 FXPS7400DS4S ----- SPI


The device is housed in a lead-free 16-pin HQFN package (4mm × 4mm × 1.98mm), 
making it well suited for compact system designs. FXPS7400D4S sensors are widely used in 
industrial environments, such as compressed air systems and manufacturing line control, 
as well as automotive applications, including comfort seating, and digital 
barometric air pressure (BAP) measurement for engine management.
