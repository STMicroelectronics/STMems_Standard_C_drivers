# MPxx5004

The MPxx5004 family (including the MPXV5004G and MPVZ5004G series) consists of 
differential and gauge integrated pressure sensors designed to measure ranges 
from 0 to 3.92kPa. These state-of-the-art monolithic silicon sensors combine advanced 
micromachining techniques, thin-film metallization, and bipolar processing to deliver 
an accurate, high-level analog output signal that is proportional to the applied 
pressure, making them well-suited for precise pressure measurement applications.

The device features temperature compensation over the range of 10°C to 60°C, ensuring
stable and reliable performance across typical operating conditions. It is available
in both gauge surface-mount (SMT) and through-hole (DIP) configurations, offering 
flexibility for different design and manufacturing requirements. The sensor is housed 
in a durable thermoplastic (PPS) package, providing mechanical robustness and resistance 
to harsh environments. 

The MPVZ5004G variant is specifically designed for appliance, consumer, healthcare, and 
industrial applications, and includes an axial port modified to accommodate
industrial-grade tubing for improved integration.

Note that all these sensors are analog output only.
